Questions clients ask

If yours isn’t here, send it over — happy to answer before you commit to anything.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with brands anywhere, keep overlapping hours with UK, EU and US time zones, and communicate over email, Slack, WhatsApp or video calls — whichever your team prefers.

Can you edit my existing theme instead of starting over?

Usually, yes. I’ll review your current Shopify theme or WordPress setup first and tell you honestly whether it’s worth extending or cheaper to rebuild.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

That’s the point of how I build. Shopify sections and ACF blocks are set up so you can change text, images and layout without code, and you get a short walkthrough video at launch.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price for clearly scoped builds, hourly for ongoing support and small fixes. Send the brief above and I’ll come back with a written estimate.

What happens after launch?

I stay on for fixes after go-live, and can continue on a monthly retainer for new features, landing pages and speed checks.
