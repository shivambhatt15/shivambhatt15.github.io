Questions clients ask

If yours isn’t here, send it over — happy to answer before you commit to anything.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with brands anywhere, keep overlapping hours with UK, EU and US time zones, and communicate over email, Slack, WhatsApp or video calls — whichever your team prefers.

Can you customise my existing Shopify theme instead of rebuilding?

Usually, yes. I’ll review your current Shopify theme or WordPress setup first and tell you honestly whether it’s worth extending or better to rebuild.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

That’s the point of how I build. Shopify sections and ACF blocks are set up so you can change text, images and layout without code, and you get a short walkthrough video at launch.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price for clearly scoped builds, and hourly or monthly retainers for ongoing support. Send the brief above and I’ll come back with a written estimate.

How long does a Shopify store take?

It depends on scope and whether designs are ready. Once we’ve talked, you get a timeline in writing with milestones you can track.

What happens after launch?

Fixes after go-live are included. Many clients continue on a monthly care plan for new sections, landing pages, updates and speed checks.
